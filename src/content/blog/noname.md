The whole world is going crazy over Spider-Man: Brand New Day. And they should be. Spider-Man is big. Spider-Man is the biggest superhero in the whole world. No one comes close to him. Some might argue that Batman and Superman do, and they are obviously wrong, and I don't argue with people like that. Because I think being a billionaire and being invulnerable and having the ability to fly, and having X-ray vision from the moment you're born, should get you on your feet pretty nicely. Sure, I dream of beating criminals up to a pulp like Batman because I am powerless and never going to have any. Anyway, that's not why I started writing this.

Would you like to know how I was introduced to Spider-Man? It's not something extraordinary, but I remember the day perfectly clearly.

I was about 10 years old. It was 2011. I was at my grandparents' (Mom's side) house for the annual school break. We didn't have electricity at our house, so I was not familiar with the whole of entertainment media. But I did like movies a lot. Back then, I used to watch Bangla movies and natoks on BTV in my village. People bought black and white tvs and used to play it on batteries.

So, I was at my grandparents’. My youngest uncle introduced me to the Star Movies channel. That channel always had a movie running. All Hollywood. I mean all English movies. That's all I knew. And those movies had crazy action. Didn't understand shit about the dialogues, though. Can you blame me? I was 10!

But I did know Superman and Batman, because I had encountered them before on some other channel. Maybe in a dubbed Hindi movie or maybe in the original, I don't remember.

I was sitting in front of tv; a new movie was about to start. The name appeared: 'Spider-Man 3'. And of course the name stuck with me. It's a Spider that is also a man. And that intro theme of the movie. Crazy good with all of these web things showing on the screen, very intense music, and different stills and arts. I instantly got hyped up.

Yeah. My first introduction to the Spider-Man character was with 'Spider-Man 3'. And I liked that movie so so much even though I didn't understand anything.

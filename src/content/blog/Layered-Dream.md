So, I was lying on my bed, sleeping. Happy that I got a new job. I could feel the happiness in my sleep. SERIOUSLY!

All of a sudden, I wake up. I don’t know why, though. The first thing I do after waking up is check for notifications. 

Oh, I got a call; it’s my friend Khairul Bashar. Odd that he called me that early in the morning. It was 7:00 am. I missed yesterday’s roster for my job, so I was sleeping through the morning. 

Anyway. I called him back. He was in the middle of traffic. He responded with, “Hey, are you awake?” 

“Yeah, what’s up?” 

“Remember that we took a quiz a couple of days ago at our University? You didn’t take it too seriously, right?”

“No, I guess not. Why? Should I have?”

“No, no! I just heard that there’s going to be a prize-giving ceremony today. And I was wondering if you got anything.”

“No.”

“qwoidhqlkqmqq,” I heard some noise, and the call got cut off. 

After the call ended, I was wondering what quiz he was talking about. I didn’t take any quiz at all. Why did I even agree that I took one? That’s odd. 

I was still lying in bed. I had all the time in the world. So, I played a song on my phone and went to sleep again. 

God Damn it! I forgot to turn off my regular alarm. I woke up with the intense sound screeching in my ears. I turned it off and went to the restroom to get refreshed. 

 I came back and heard a couple of kids playing outside. Then a sudden ‘Thump!’ I looked up and saw that one of the kids jumped on the side of my window. He could literally look through the glass window and see that I was ‘*NAKED’*! Why was I naked?  

I quickly picked up the blanket from the floor and wrapped it around me. God, this is so embarrassing. 

I shout, “Hey, kid…What the hell do you think you’re doing?” 

“Mr., just give me a minute, and I will get down. Something is going on on the roof, and I am scared.” 

“Kid, I am scared of losing my dignity in your eyes. Get off my window! Get the hell off, now!”

After the kid got off, I looked out through the window and properly at the roof outside. Now, that was odd. 

There’s a motorcycle standing right outside with two guys sitting on top. One of them was holding a mobile phone in his right ear, carefully checking out my window, and it seemed like he was reporting to someone about the whole situation. 

I needed to know what was going on. 

“Hello! Do you want something?” 

“Yes! Are you Hasan Imam Mithun?” 

“Yes, I am.”

“You should expect a visitor. You got into something you shouldn’t have.”

I was a little more curious than scared. I haven’t done anything to anybody lately. Why would I be in trouble? I said, “Okay!” and shut the windows properly.

It was around 8:00 am. I was sitting on my laptop, writing down the whole thing. After all, it was a weird morning, and I got to write it. And I didn’t even mention the motorcycle on the rooftop. How the hell did they even get it there? All kinds of questions were popping up in my mind. Why was I in trouble? How did they know my name? What were the kids doing? My window is at least 7 feet high from the next building’s rooftop. And there’s no place to put your feet on. How were the kids even hanging there? 

*Knock! Knock! Knock!*

Really! That early. No one on earth who knows me would visit me that early. One, because the ones who know where I live are no longer in Dhaka, or have a job. Two, they don’t have the key to the main gate. 

If it were the 5th-floor neighbor, I swore that I’d kill her. Push her off the stairs. 

That lying piece of crap told my landlord that I smoked on the rooftop while I haven’t smoked a day in my life in my 24 freaking years of life. She made a fuss multiple times over this matter. So, one night, I went to the roof around 10:30 at night to investigate. 

And he was right there. A 70-year-old man was smoking right there. I asked him which floor he lived on. The man turned out to be the brother of the old lady. 

I took photos of the cigar filter after he left and sent them to my landlord. Glad to say, there were no complaints again after that event. 

I opened the door. And there he was. It was Abir Mahmud Jim. My high school friend, with a serious, stern look on his face, asked me if he could come in.

I said, “Of course, come in!” I still couldn’t believe what was going on. Why would he visit me? I hadn’t seen him for nearly 4 to 5 years, I thought. 

“Jim, what brings you here?” Right at the moment I asked him the question, I remembered something.

Jim was supposed to be in the UK for his studies. He was married, and most importantly, he was in the UK. There was no way that he would be right in front of me. 

So, I confronted him. “Jim, tell me the truth! Is this a dream?” 

The look on his face, man! He got so confused and angry that he got caught. He told me the truth, though. It was a dream. 

And I woke up. 

Imagine Dragons’ ‘Bleeding Out’ was playing on my phone. And it was 7:20 in the morning. 

And right now, it’s exactly 8:00 AM as I am finishing this. Thank God there’s no knock on my door!

*Knock! Knock! Knock!*

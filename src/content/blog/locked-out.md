Da da da da!…..Not just any Da da da da. Sheldon Cooper-level Da da da da.  I woke up at 6:00 in the morning. It’s Friday. I had a good sleep. Last night was productive. It’s been a stressful week, but I am happy. Happy that I am not that miserable anymore. 

I watched reels on Instagram for half an hour, and then got up, took my toothbrush, shorts, towel, and soup to take a shower and get ready to go to the office. Oh, what a wonderful day! The sky was clear. The sun was not yet shining, as it was only 6:30. But I was sure it would. What could go wrong on such a wonderful day! 

INDEED….What could go wrong? 

I went out of the room and pulled the door behind me. And what happened after that moment, I felt it in slow motion. I experienced it at 5 frames per second. At least, that’s what I believed.

I didn’t even take one step forward.

As soon as I pulled the door, I realized that I didn’t have my keys in my hand, and I had pressed the lock before I pulled the door. By the time I looked back, it was too late. To my horror, I was locked out of my room. At 6:30 in the morning. With nothing but a towel and a toothbrush in my hand. 

All I could do was stare at the locked knob intensely and hope that, by some miracle, the door would open. Alas, that would never be possible in a million years. 

Right from that moment, I started thinking about every decision I ever took in my life that led me to this situation. As I sat on the commode, I kept wondering, kept contemplating the misery of my life. 

There was no point in taking a shower anymore. I brushed my teeth and got out of the restroom. I went out of the apartment’s main door(kept the door completely open, in case I locked this one too) and sat on the stairs.

I kept sitting there with nothing but my thoughts. And it was the worst. Yes, it was the worst feeling in the world. Every moment of my life kept coming back to my mind like a tsunami. I kept trying to think about some happy memories, something that would distract me. But nothing. 

My two failed relationships, me failing to get into a public university, me not doing well academically in my entire life, me not working hard enough to publish a paper, me not developing my skills enough, me not getting a good job after graduation, and every other thing that led me here. 

Out of my room. Without my mobile and laptop. Yes, it was the worst morning of my life. I got up. Kept walking back and forth in the dining room. I couldn’t sit still anymore. 

I live on the sixth floor. It was hot here. Combine that heat with my restless thoughts. I was sweating like I just ran 10 km. I eventually got tired and went to the stairs to sit again. 

I was standing in front of the stairs when I heard a ‘meow’. I looked down and saw the cat from downstairs coming up. I asked him what he wanted. He didn’t care to answer. Didn’t even say a meow. 

He slowly came up and sat at the exact position I was sitting a moment ago. At the exact position. I was furious. I told him, “Dude, I was sitting there! What’s your problem?” 

I was left with no answer again. I had no choice but to sit in a different position. 

The cat and I were face to face now. As he took my seat, I intended to give him a piece of my mind. I stared at him with such intensity in my eyes without blinking; I thought he would get scared and give up the seat. But this cat started doing the same. It looked into my eyes, and I could only assume that he was challenging me in a staring contest. 

So I stared at him more intensely. No one is blinking. No one is saying anything. I was losing this battle. It was a freaking cat. He would never lose a staring contest. 

I finally blinked and accepted my defeat. Maybe it was 5 minutes or so; I heard the cat make another meow sound. I looked up at him and saw that he was getting up. He came right beside me. Gave me a couple of rubs on my thigh, sat beside me for a moment, and did a couple of ‘meows’. 

And soothed me. 

I was distracted from my thoughts. I didn’t know how much time had passed since I was locked out of my room. I got up and saw that my neighbour had woken up. I asked him to call my landlord to find out if he had any extra keys to the room. 

Luckily, he had. He let me know the location of his building. I walked about half a kilometer and got the key. And when I finally got back inside my room, it was 9:30 in the morning. I missed my office for the day.

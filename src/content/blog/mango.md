As Mithun entered Sakib's room, he looked around and saw a bag full of mangoes. And he thought to himself, 'Wow! Mangoes!" And of course, he wanted to enjoy them. Before that, he wanted to have lunch.

He and Sakib both had an excellent lunch with lentil soup and egg fry. They ate so much, Sakib thought he couldn't eat another grain of rice. So, when Mithun offered him some mangoes, he rejected them (What an idiot!). Then Mithun thought to himself, 'Oh yeah, baby! I am going to enjoy these mangoes so much, their seeds will become white from my licking." So, he took the full bag. He counted, and there were six mangoes. And he only wanted to have two of them because he was already full. But that's never stopped Mithun from overeating, he reminded himself. But he was considerate. He thought, "I should keep some for Sovon and Sakib", and then he smirked because there was no way that was going to happen.

Mithun brought a knife and a bowl and sat in front of his laptop. He was so going to enjoy the mangoes with a movie. He cut the first mango and saw that it was a bit rotten on the front. He was still hopeful that the rest of the mango was good; he could eat it. And he started peeling the mango by hand. As he made his way to the back of the mango, he became more disappointed because there was no way he could eat that. He threw the whole mango in the bowl.

He took another mango, and luckily, its face wasn't rotten, and it smelled so nice. Mithun became happy. He quickly started to peel the rest of the mango, and just as he reached the middle, he found that the mango was rotten black from there. And it smelled so bad, he almost threw up.

He quickly picked up another mango. He already started thinking that if his luck really was that bad that all of those mangoes would be rotten. He hoped not. He again could not eat the third mango. He didn't even have to cut the fourth one to know that it was bad.

There were two mangoes, still. Mithun wanted to give up, but he still was craving the taste of sweet, juicy mangoes on his tongue.

He picked up the fifth mango from the bag, and as he did, his face and eyes were beginning to fill with dread and disappointment. He just knew it would be bad. But the fifth mango surprised him. It really did. Oh no, no, he still couldn't eat it, it was rotten. But it was a different kind of rotten. Mithun thought he smelled the smell of ripe jackfruit inside the rotten mango. He didn't know how it was possible, but at this point, he wasn't going to question his judgment or his senses. Anything was possible.

Mithun picked up the sixth mango, he cut the front, and after cutting it, he looked at the back and saw that it was black. He wasn't surprised; he knew it. But he was a little bit sad. He went to the kitchen with the bowl full of mangoes and put them in a polythene bag. He washed the bowl, put a knot on the polythene, washed the knife, and came back in front of the laptop.

Of course, he didn't have any mangoes, he was sad, and of course, he asked himself if it was really worth living if he couldn't have just one mango? Why would God be that unfair to him?

So what? He was still happy that his right hand smelled like ripe mango. There is a saying in Sanskrit, which says 'ঘ্রাণেনৈব ভোজনং', which means 'Half the meal is just the smell.' So, he sat in front of the laptop and smelt his hand for five minute before he started writing this shitty story of 'How to Not Have Any Mango Even When You Have Mango?'
